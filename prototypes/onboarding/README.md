# Cortex onboarding prototype

Open `index.html` in Chrome. No build step.

- **Picker (top):** switch direction. Seen · Ask · Wonder. Keys `1–3` or `←/→`. `↻` or `R` replays.
- **Left panel:** switch persona (Riya, Sam, Meera, Dev) at any step. The same screen re-renders with their content.
- **Right panel:** the journey map, live. What this persona thinks and feels at this step, the job it serves, where their wow lands, and how the other three feel. Click any step to jump. `B` / `N` step back and forward.
- **Tap the brain.** It ripples.

Simulated: ChatGPT import, sign-in, payments, notifications. Prices are placeholders.

Debug: `?v=1&p=sam&s=7&still=1` opens Seen, Sam, step 7 with animations off (for screenshots). `&click=<css selector>` clicks something after load.
