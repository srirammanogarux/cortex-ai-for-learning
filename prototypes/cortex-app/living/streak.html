How the streak works

Any basic exploration counts: a finished lesson or one Ask question. The first one each day lights the flame. Until then it shrinks and dims through the day, and from 6 PM it’s at risk. Freezes: hold up to 2, earned at 7, 14, 30 and 100 days, used for you automatically, never sold. Winning a streak back is never paywalled.

Every tier

It grows with the streak: day 1, 7, 30, 100 and 365. Before day 1 it waits, ready to start.

At 24 px, in the chip

The real top-bar counter at 24 px. The chip’s tint carries the state too: ember when lit, a saffron ring at risk, frost when frozen, a dashed edge to win back. The lit numeral needs no icon: its digits glow. Returning users never see 0.

The words

Plain, kind, short. Never “lost”, never “0”, never a guilt trip.
