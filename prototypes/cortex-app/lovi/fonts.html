Which free font is closest to ES Rebond Grotesque?

Lovi sets its headlines in ES Rebond Grotesque by Extraset (Regular and Medium, about 44–48px, −1px tracking) and its body in Geist with stylistic sets ss01, ss02 and ss06. Rebond is commercial, and its trial licence rules out our public site and repo. The rule: use a free lookalike only if it is about 99% the same.

Aa

Verdict: no free font is 99% Rebond. Hanken Grotesk is the closest, at about 85%.

Hanken Grotesk on its variable weight axis (Regular ≈ 380, Medium ≈ 480, −0.022em) matches Rebond’s colour, x-height, width and its t, y, g, R and C. It scored best on ink overlap too. What gives it away up close:

  • Rebond’s ? and 2 have a calligraphic curl; Hanken’s are plain.
  • Rebond’s a has a smaller, tucked bowl, and its terminals are slightly flared.
  • Rebond’s spacing is a touch more even at large sizes.

So the public Lovi build uses Hanken Grotesk as a stand-in, wired through one variable, --lovi-display. For the real look, put the Rebond trial files in lovi/fonts/ (git-ignored) on a private deploy, uncomment the @font-face stub in lovi/theme.css, and point --lovi-display at it.

How it was scored

Each candidate drew the same three lines (“Hi, I’m Cortex”, “What do you want to learn?” and “Hamburgefonstiv agtyR 0123”) in the browser. Each line was cropped to its ink, scaled to Rebond’s box, and compared pixel by pixel. The score is the average shared ink (intersection over union). A font that is the same would score above 0.9. Weight and tracking were tuned per font by eye first. Geist, Lovi’s body face, is included for scale.
