# Onboarding import prompt (V0 draft v2)

The user copies this from Cortex onboarding, pastes it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, then pastes the answer back.

It only works if their assistant can see past chats (memory or chat history turned on). Otherwise, fall back to one of two options:
- a screenshot of their chat list
- the bubble picker

```
Look back at our past conversations and what you remember about me.
I want a map of my curiosity, not my tasks.

1. INTERESTS: my top 5–7 curiosity areas, and anything you remember I'm into.
2. QUESTIONS: up to 20 things I asked to learn or understand something
   (skip tasks like emails, code fixes, trip plans). For each:
   topic | my question in my own words | how deep I went (quick / a few follow-ups / long session)
   | one line on how you answered (e.g. "long text explanation", "bullet list", "step-by-step")
3. STYLE: how I seem to like things explained (examples, analogies, visuals, numbers, short vs detailed).
4. UNFINISHED: 3 questions I keep circling but never fully explored.
5. FAVOURITE: the one exploration I seemed to enjoy most. Give my original question
   and a 3-line summary of the answer you gave me.

Reply as plain text under the heading CORTEX PROFILE. No intro, no advice.
```

## How Cortex uses it
1. **Curiosity map.** Interests become clusters and questions become bubbles; the size of each bubble shows how deep they went. The map assembles itself in front of them. The user can remove any bubble (trust and privacy).
2. **Before / after.** Take FAVOURITE or an UNFINISHED question. Show "what you got" (their old text answer, shrunk) next to it growing into a Cortex Exploration. Their own question, answered better, is the wow.
3. **STYLE** sets the lead-card preference. **UNFINISHED** seeds the "sparks" on the Ask canvas and on Today.
