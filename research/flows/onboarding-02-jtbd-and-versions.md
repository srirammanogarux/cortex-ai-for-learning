# Onboarding · Step 2: Jobs to be done in depth, then diverge into onboarding versions

Status: draft for review (2026-11-27). Builds on `onboarding-01-personas-jtbd.md`.

---

## A. The core job: "Satisfy a curiosity"

### The job map
The same job every time curiosity strikes, broken into steps. ChatGPT only serves steps 2–3. Cortex wins on 3–7.

| # | Job step | What the person is trying to do | How ChatGPT does it | Where Cortex can win |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Notice** | Something sparks a question (reel, object, meeting, class) | Doesn't help | Sparks, real-world input (photo, link), reminders of open questions |
| 2 | **Capture** | Ask before the moment passes | Good: open the app, type | Equal: one tap, voice, share sheet from any app |
| 3 | **Understand** | Get the "aha", not just information | Wall of text | **Visual, interactive, explained your way** |
| 4 | **Explore further** | Follow the thread that pulls next | Scroll back, re-prompt | **Rabbit holes, branches, trail** |
| 5 | **Make it stick** | Remember it next week | Nothing | **Practice, spaced review, brain map** |
| 6 | **Use / share** | Explain it, apply it, send it | Copy and paste text | **Shareable cards, "explain it back", deep dives with sources** |
| 7 | **Return** | Pick up open threads later | Chat history nobody reopens | **Memory canvas, "you never finished…", day-2 hook** |

**Onboarding must show step 3 plus at least one of steps 4–7 inside the first minute.** That's the gap ChatGPT can't cover.

### Desired outcomes, which double as success criteria
How people judge the job, written as "minimise" or "maximise":

- **Understand (step 3)**
  - Minimise the time from question to "I get it" (under 60s).
  - Minimise the chance it's explained in a way that doesn't fit how I think.
  - Maximise how sure I am it's correct (sources for the Builder).
- **Explore further (step 4)**
  - Minimise effort to go one level deeper.
  - Maximise how interesting the next thread feels.
- **Make it stick (step 5)**
  - Maximise how much I remember a week later.
  - Minimise the effort of revising.
- **Use / share (step 6)**
  - Maximise how easily I can explain it to someone else.
  - Maximise how good it looks when shared.
- **Return (step 7)**
  - Minimise the chance a curiosity is lost.
  - Minimise the effort to resume.

### Persona emphasis on the job map

| Step | Wanderer | Builder | Student |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Notice | ★★★ | ★ | ★ |
| 2 Capture | ★★ | ★★ | ★★ |
| 3 Understand | ★★ | ★★★ | ★★★ |
| 4 Explore further | ★★★ | ★★ | ★ |
| 5 Make it stick | ★ | ★★ | ★★★ |
| 6 Use / share | ★★★ (share) | ★★★ (explain, apply) | ★★ (prove in exam) |
| 7 Return | ★★ | ★★★ (multi-day) | ★★ |

### The emotional and social jobs behind the functional job
- **Wanderer:** feel curious, delighted and interesting; be the person who knows cool things.
- **Builder:** feel competent and ahead; never be caught out in a meeting.
- **Student:** feel smart instead of stuck; feel ready.

### The anxieties onboarding has to defuse
- **"Another app I'll abandon."** Show value before asking for anything.
- **"Is it accurate?"** Sources and confidence, especially for the Builder.
- **"Is this a kids' / school app?"** Premium tone, no levels, no cartoon.
- **"Will it read my private chats?"** The import has to be optional, transparent and editable, with a founder-letter-style promise.
- **"Paywall trap."** Value first, a soft paywall, and a clear free tier.

## B. Diverge: eight onboarding versions

Each version gives its premise, its steps, **its wow**, the job steps it proves, and its cost.

### V1 · Import & Reveal (Quest-style)
- **Steps:**
  1. Brain intro.
  2. "Where do you go when you're curious?"
  3. Import from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini (a copy-paste prompt, or a screenshot of the chat list).
  4. An understanding meter fills.
  5. **Curiosity Wrapped** reveal chapters: "You're a rabbit-holer", "38 questions were pure curiosity", "the one you keep coming back to".
  6. The brain lights up its regions.
  7. "You never finished: *Why do we dream?*" → before/after → Exploration.
- **Wow:** *being seen.* "It knows me better than I expected."
- **Proves job steps:** 7 (return), 3 (understand).
- **Cost:** high friction (leaving the app, pasting). It only works for ChatGPT users with history. Privacy anxiety.
- **Best for:** heavy ChatGPT users, any persona.

### V2 · Ask first (zero onboarding, ChatGPT-native)
- **Steps:**
  1. Land straight on the brain with "What are you curious about?" and 3 tailored sparks.
  2. Type, speak or tap.
  3. The brain thinks, then forms the topic.
  4. Exploration.
  5. Personalisation is asked *after* the wow, inline.
- **Wow:** *instant magic.* "I asked, and it *drew* the answer."
- **Proves job steps:** 2, 3.
- **Cost:** a generic first answer (no personalisation yet), and the reveal is smaller.
- **Best for:** impatient users, the Builder.

### V3 · Spark rain (Wabi-style)
- **Steps:**
  1. Glossy curiosity spheres rain down, each a question with an illustration.
  2. "Tap the ones that pull you" (pick 3).
  3. They fly into the brain, which absorbs them and changes colour.
  4. It builds the first Exploration from your favourite.
- **Wow:** *playful discovery.* "Every one of these looks interesting."
- **Proves job steps:** 1, 4.
- **Cost:** shallow personalisation, and it can feel like a content-picker.
- **Best for:** the Wanderer.

### V4 · Taste test ("Which explanation clicks?")
- **Steps:**
  1. One intriguing question.
  2. Three quick explanation cards: story, diagram, analogy. Swipe and tap the one that clicks.
  3. "Got it. You think in pictures."
  4. Ask your own question, explained your way.
- **Wow:** *"It explains the way my brain works."*
- **Proves job steps:** 3, plus the personalisation promise.
- **Cost:** adds a step before the user's own question.
- **Best for:** the Student and the Wanderer.

### V5 · Conversation with the brain (Wondering-style)
- **Steps:**
  1. The brain speaks: 3 quick questions (curious about what? why do you explore? how deep?), each acknowledged in voice and text.
  2. A live build ("Drawing the heart… writing the aha…").
  3. "Your first exploration is ready" → sign in → it opens.
- **Wow:** *a tutor tailoring things for me in real time.*
- **Proves job steps:** 3.
- **Cost:** feels like a form if the acknowledgements are weak. Slower.
- **Best for:** all personas; the safest option.

### V6 · Point at the world (snap / link to learn)
- **Steps:**
  1. "Show me something you're curious about."
  2. Camera (an object, a plant, a building, a page), paste a link (reel, YouTube, article), or share from another app.
  3. The brain studies it → forms → Exploration about *that thing*.
- **Wow:** *"I can point at anything and understand it."* Curiosity from the real world.
- **Proves job steps:** 1, 2, 3, which ChatGPT can't match for flow.
- **Cost:** needs strong vision quality and camera permission. A weak result is a bad first impression.
- **Best for:** the Wanderer, and anyone in the moment.

### V7 · Guess first (surprise hook)
- **Steps:**
  1. A surprising question with a slider or 3 options ("Your heart pumps how much blood a day?").
  2. Guess.
  3. Reveal: "7,500 litres. A swimming pool a week."
  4. The brain forms the heart → Exploration.
  5. "Ask your own".
- **Wow:** *the delight of being surprised.* Being wrong in a fun way (the pretesting effect).
- **Proves job steps:** 3, 5 (it hints at practice).
- **Cost:** our question, not theirs.
- **Best for:** the Student and the Wanderer.

### V8 · Bring your work (project-led)
- **Steps:**
  1. "Learning for something?" → paste what you're working on (a meeting agenda, job description, syllabus, doc).
  2. The brain maps the concepts you'll need, as a lit-up brain with gaps.
  3. "Start with the biggest gap: *Interest rates & startups*" → Exploration with sources.
- **Wow:** *"It knows exactly what I need for tomorrow."*
- **Proves job steps:** 3, 6, 7.
- **Cost:** only relevant when there's a concrete project; heavier input.
- **Best for:** the Builder, and the Student with a syllabus.

## D. Combinations to prototype (3–4 versions, each varying one big idea)
All share: brain-first visuals, sign-in after the wow, the paywall at the rabbit-holes screen, and the first Exploration tailored to the detected persona.

### Prototype 1 · "Seen" (import-led)
**Flow:** V1 → V4-lite.
1. Brain intro.
2. Import (or skip).
3. Meter.
4. Wrapped reveal (3 chapters).
5. Taste test on *their own* unfinished question.
6. Before/after.
7. Exploration.
8. Rabbit hole.
9. Paywall → sign in.

**Fallback if they skip the import:** drop into Prototype 2's sparks.
**Tests:** is being seen worth the friction?

### Prototype 2 · "Instant" (ask-led)
**Flow:** V2 + V7 + V1 offered later.
1. Brain + "What are you curious about?" + sparks, plus one guess-first spark ("Guess: how much blood does your heart pump a day?").
2. Exploration.
3. Rabbit hole.
4. "Want Cortex to know what you're curious about? Bring your ChatGPT questions" (optional, with a meter).
5. Paywall → sign in.

**Tests:** does a 15-second wow beat a 90-second reveal?

### Prototype 3 · "Real world" (world-led)
**Flow:** V6 + V3 fallback.
1. "Show me something you're curious about": camera, link or share.
2. If none, spark rain (pick 3).
3. The brain studies it and forms the answer.
4. Exploration.
5. Rabbit hole.
6. Paywall.

**Tests:** is "point at anything" the differentiating hook?

### Prototype 4 (optional) · "For work and study" (goal-led)
**Flow:** V5-lite + V8.
1. Two questions (why do you explore? how deep?).
2. Paste your project or syllabus.
3. The brain shows concept gaps.
4. An Exploration with sources on the biggest gap.
5. Deep-dive paywall.

**Tests:** does a goal-led entry convert Builders and Students better?
