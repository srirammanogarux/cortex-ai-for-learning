# Onboarding v2 · The wow is the idea map

Status: ideation (2026-11-27). Builds on `onboarding-08-trailer-flow.md` and the Wondering teardown.

## The wow, redefined
There are two parts:
1. **“How does it know?”** The curiosity questions are uncannily specific to your world: your role, your syllabus, your interests.
2. **“Everything connects.”** Your question opens into a living map of linked ideas. You can scroll, drag and fly between them freely, and each idea opens into its own visual. The path you take becomes your memory.

## The idea map (core mechanic)
- **Centre:** your question, as the living visual the brain morphed into.
- **Around it:** 5–8 linked ideas. Every link has a type and a one-line reason:

| Link type | Meaning | Example (PM: “Why do some products become habits?”) |
|---|---|---|
| Why | the cause underneath | Dopamine spikes *before* the reward, not after |
| Deeper | the mechanism | Variable rewards: why slot machines work |
| Same idea in | the same pattern in another field | Why you check your phone 96× a day |
| Leads to | the consequence | Why retention curves flatten |
| For you | applied to your role, exam or life | How to design a habit loop into onboarding |
| Try it | a check or puzzle | Spot the habit loop in this product |

- **Moving around:**
  - Drag to pan, pinch to zoom.
  - Tap an idea and the camera flies to it, and it opens into its visual.
  - Swipe to follow the strongest link.
  - Your path lights up behind you (your trail), and it gets saved to Memory.
- **How the map changes by branch:**
  - Work → applications to your role.
  - Exams → prerequisites, the next topic, and likely exam questions.
  - Sharpen → mental models plus puzzles.
  - Curious → surprising jumps across subjects.

## The four branches

### Work
- **Step 1:** role (engineer, PM, designer, founder, marketing, finance, researcher, teacher, other).
- **Step 2:** “What’s on your plate?” (optional, one tap). For a PM: discovery, launching, growth, team.
- **Questions** (PM example):
  - How do you know a customer really wants it?
  - Why do some products become habits?
  - How do you decide what *not* to build?
  - Why do most launches flatline in week 2?
- **Map:** the ideas behind it, with “For you” links back to your work.

### Exams / school
- **Step 1:** level (school class, college year, or a competitive exam: JEE, NEET, UPSC, SAT, GRE…).
- **Step 2:** subject.
- **Step 3:** chapter or topic, or “Snap a page / past paper”.
- **Questions** (Class 12 Physics example):
  - Why does a moving magnet make current?
  - Why does current lag voltage in an inductor?
  - Why can’t a transformer work on DC?
  - Why is the sky blue but sunsets red?
- **Map:** the chapter as a web. Prerequisites behind it, next topics ahead, real-world uses beside it, and “Try it” exam questions on the edges.
- **The wow:** “I can see how the whole chapter connects.”

### Sharpen my mind
- **Step 1:** what you want sharper: decisions, logic, numbers, memory, spotting patterns, big ideas.
- **Questions:**
  - Why do we overvalue what we already own?
  - How do you think in probabilities?
  - Why do memory palaces work?
  - Can you spot the flaw in this argument?
- **Map:** a web of mental models. Each idea links to a related model, plus a puzzle to try it on.
- **The wow:** “Ideas I can use, and I just used one.”

### Just curious
- **Step 1:** pick the subjects you love (glowing orbs).
- **Questions:**
  - Why can’t you tickle yourself?
  - How big is a blue whale’s heart?
  - Why is Jaipur pink?
  - What’s at the bottom of the ocean?
- **Map:** surprising jumps across subjects. Tickling → your brain predicts your own touch → why your recorded voice sounds weird → why placebos work.
- **The wow:** “I didn’t know these were connected.”

### Bring your own (every branch)
Type a question, paste a link or doc, snap a page, or connect ChatGPT (“I’ll find what you never finished”).

## How questions become uncannily specific
Generated from: goal + role or level + subjects + (optionally) ChatGPT history. The rules:
- **Use real nouns from their world:** a PRD, Class 12 Physics, a JEE paper, a sprint.
- **Phrase it as a puzzle or tension** (“why…”, “how do you know…”, “why can’t…”), never as a course title.
- **Mix three kinds:** something they need now, something they’ve always wondered, and one surprise.
