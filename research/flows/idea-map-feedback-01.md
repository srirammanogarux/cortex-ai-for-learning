# Idea map prototype · feedback round 1 (2026-11-27)

Live: https://cortex-idea-map.vercel.app · code: `prototypes/idea-map/`

The idea comes across. The execution is rough. To fix when we come back to the map, after onboarding:

1. **Cluttered.** Too much on screen at once.
2. **Zoom in / zoom out doesn’t feel right.**
3. **Node connections don’t read well.**
4. **Remove the separate “Dive in” button.** Tapping the centre is enough.
5. **“Ask your own question” doesn’t work.**
6. **Bottom chips:** it isn’t obvious that they scroll sideways, or what they’re for.
7. **Search:** unclear why it’s there.
8. **Top-right trail counter** (dot + number) is meaningless to users.
9. **The user’s question** (“Why do some products become habits?”) should stay visible as a chat bubble.
10. **Not fluid overall.** It needs real, smooth interactivity, not a rough sketch.

Decision: build onboarding first, then return to the map.
