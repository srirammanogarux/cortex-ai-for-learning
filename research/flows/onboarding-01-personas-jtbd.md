# Onboarding · Step 1: Personas, jobs to be done, and what onboarding must prove

Status: draft for review (2026-11-27). V0 personas only. The Specialist is out of scope until V1.

## The shared situation
All three already open ChatGPT (or Claude or Gemini) when something makes them curious. They get a wall of text, skim it, and forget it. Nothing connects one question to the next, and nothing brings them back. **Cortex has to beat that habit within the first minute**, or they go back to ChatGPT.

## Personas and their jobs

### Riya, the Wanderer (27, designer, Bengaluru)
- **Triggers:** a reel, a documentary, a conversation, travel. Evening scrolling, weekends.
- **Main job:** *"When something sparks my curiosity, help me understand it in a way that feels delightful, so I get that 'whoa' and can share it."*
- **Related jobs:**
  - "Remind me of things I wondered about and dropped."
  - "Let me wander from one thing to the next without planning."
- **Emotional job:** feel interesting and in the know.
- **Social job:** have something to send to friends.
- **She'd leave if:** it feels like school (quizzes up front, levels, homework); it's slow; or it's just ChatGPT with a nicer skin.
- **What a wow means to her:** *"I've never seen it explained like that."* Beauty plus surprise, and an urge to share.
- **What activation looks like:** she follows at least one rabbit hole out of pure curiosity, and screenshots or shares a card.

### Sam, the Builder (34, PM, Austin; and Arjun-type founders in India)
- **Triggers:** a meeting term, a podcast, a new project or domain. Commute, pre-meeting, focused sessions.
- **Main job:** *"When I need to get up to speed on something for work, give me a mental model I can use tomorrow, fast, and one I can trust."*
- **Related jobs:**
  - "Go deeper over a few days without losing the thread."
  - "Let me explain it to someone else."
- **Emotional job:** feel competent, not caught out.
- **He'd leave if:** it's fluffy or cute, he can't see sources, it wastes his time with gamification, or it doesn't fit his context.
- **What a wow means to him:** *"That's exactly the mental model I needed. And it used my world as the example."* Precision plus personalisation.
- **What activation looks like:** he finishes one Exploration on a work topic, and opens either "Make it a deep dive" or a cited source.

### Meera, the Student (19, engineering, Pune)
- **Triggers:** a concept she doesn't get from class, exam prep, side curiosity. Late evenings, before exams.
- **Main job:** *"When a concept doesn't click, help me actually understand it, and prove to me that I do."*
- **Related jobs:**
  - "Make revision less painful."
  - "Let me check myself before the exam."
- **Emotional job:** stop feeling stupid; feel confident.
- **She'd leave if:** it's paywalled before any value, it's too shallow for exams, or it has no practice.
- **What a wow means to her:** *"Oh, NOW I get it."* An interactive aha (she drags it and sees it), then gets the check question right.
- **What activation looks like:** she interacts with the aha diagram, answers the quick check correctly, and saves it for practice.

## What every onboarding must prove, in order
1. **"It knows what I'm curious about."** Personal from second one. Import, or one tap of choice.
2. **"It explains better than ChatGPT."** Their own question, shown side by side with a visible upgrade.
3. **"It's alive."** The brain reacts, thinks and forms the answer. Not a form to fill in.
4. **"I want more."** A rabbit hole, a deep dive, or a check question. Then the paywall arrives at peak desire.

## How onboarding tells the personas apart (no explicit "pick your persona")
| Signal | Wanderer | Builder | Student |
|---|---|---|---|
| Imported questions | Varied, "why does…", pop culture, nature | Work terms, "how does X affect Y", tools | Course concepts, "explain…", exam terms |
| Answer to "Why do you explore?" | For the joy | For work | For study |
| Preferred explanation (shown 3 ways, they pick one) | Story | Model/diagram, then source | Interactive + check |

## How onboarding and activation adapt to each persona
| | Wanderer | Builder | Student |
|---|---|---|---|
| First question chosen | The most *delightful* unfinished question | The most *recent work* question | The concept they asked about *most* |
| Lead card | Story trail with an illustration | Model or versus, with sources visible | Living model you interact with |
| Tone of copy | Playful, surprising | Direct, efficient | Encouraging, clear |
| End of first Exploration | 3 rabbit holes + "Share this" | "Make it a deep dive" + sources | Quick check → "Add to practice" |
| Paywall pitch | "Keep wandering. Unlimited explorations" | "Go deep on anything. Deep dives + sources" | "Understand everything for your exams. Unlimited + practice" |
| Day-2 hook | "A spark you'll like" notification | "Part 2 of rates & startups is ready" | "3 concepts to check before your exam" |

## Success metrics for judging prototype versions
- **Time to first wow** (the brain forms their topic): under 60 seconds.
- **Activation in the first session:** finish Exploration 1, interact with the diagram, and follow one rabbit hole.
- **Trial start at the paywall.**
- **Import opt-in rate,** and drop-off at each step.

## Open questions for step 2
1. Is import the **default first step** (Quest-style, high wow but more friction), or **offered after** a first instant wow from a tapped spark (low friction)?
2. Is "Why do you explore?" asked as an explicit question, or **inferred** from the import and only confirmed?
3. Is the before/after ChatGPT comparison shown **for everyone**, or only for people who imported?
