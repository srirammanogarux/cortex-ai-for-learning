# Riya, the Wanderer: jobs in depth through the question lens

Status: draft for review (2026-11-27). This brings back the earlier mental model:

> **Response format = Question intent × Concept shape × Person**
> - **Intent:** how · why · what · compare · what's happening · what if
> - **Shape:** mechanism · story/history · abstraction · quantity/scale · place · comparison
> - **Person:** Wanderer / Builder / Student, plus how they like things explained
> - Together these pick the **lead card**: living model · story trail · analogy flip · scale zoom · map · versus · myth vs fact

The jobs are defined *by the questions she actually asks*. Each kind of question is its own small job with its own "whoa".

---

## 1. Who Riya is when she's curious
- **Curiosity engine:** noticing. She sees, hears or eats something, and a "wait, why?" appears.
- **Where it comes from (triggers):**
  - Reels and shorts
  - Documentaries
  - Travel and walks
  - Food and cooking
  - Design and everyday objects
  - Nature and animals
  - Conversations and pop culture
  - Random shower thoughts
- **Moments:** evening couch time, weekends, right after the trigger (within about 60 seconds, or it's gone), and sometimes while travelling.
- **Appetite:** wide, not deep. 2–5 minutes per topic, then she hops to the next. One question often chains into 3–4 others.
- **What she wants back:** a feeling first, understanding second. "Whoa", "no way", "huh, that makes sense", "aww".
- **What she does after:** sends it to a friend or partner, brings it up at dinner, saves it for later.

## 3. One job per question type, with the right response

| Question type | Riya's micro-job ("When…, I want…, so…") | The whoa she wants | Lead card | Rabbit holes that pull her | Share artifact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Why × mechanism** ("Why do cats purr?") | When I notice something odd in everyday life, I want to see what's going on inside it, so it suddenly makes sense | "Ohhh, *that's* why" | **Living model:** tap the throat, feel the purr frequency | "Can purring heal bones?" · "Why don't big cats purr?" | A looping animation card |
| **Why × story** ("Why are train coaches blue and red?") | When something ordinary has a hidden backstory, I want the story behind it, so I can tell it at dinner | "No way, that's the reason?" | **Story trail:** 3–5 photo beats on a timeline or map | "Why are Mumbai taxis black and yellow?" | A "Did you know" story card |
| **Why × abstraction** ("Why do we find faces beautiful?") | When I wonder about myself or people, I want an idea I can picture, so I understand myself a bit better | "Huh, that's me" | **Analogy flip:** analogy first, then the science | "Why do babies like faces?" | A quote-style insight card |
| **Why/how × scale** ("How big is a blue whale's heart?") | When something is unimaginably big, small or fast, I want to *feel* the size, so it actually sinks in | "WHAT?!" | **Scale zoom:** you next to it; drag to compare | "Biggest animal that ever lived?" | A side-by-side scale card |
| **How × mechanism** ("How do octopuses change colour?") | When something seems like magic, I want to watch it work step by step, so the magic becomes wonder | "That's so cool" | **Living model** with a scrubber | "Can octopuses see colour?" | An animated GIF card |
| **What if** ("What if the moon disappeared?") | When my imagination wanders, I want to play the scenario out, so I see a world I can't otherwise see | "That's terrifying / amazing" | **Scenario slider:** drag time; the world changes | "What if Earth had rings?" | A before/after card |
| **Myth** ("Do we only use 10% of our brain?") | When I hear a "fact", I want to know if it's true, so I don't repeat nonsense, and so I can correct friends | "I KNEW it" / "wait, really?" | **Myth vs fact:** guess first, then the reveal | "Other brain myths" | A "Myth busted" card |
| **What is** ("What is umami?") | When I hear a word I half-know, I want to grasp it in one go, so I can use it confidently | "Ah, so that's what it means" | **Analogy flip** or **living model** | "Why does MSG have a bad rep?" | A definition card |

**Shared across her jobs:**
- **Capture fast** (within about 60s of the trigger: share from Instagram or YouTube, voice).
- **Always another thread:** 3 rabbit holes ranked by "surprise".
- **Something worth sending** at the end of every Exploration.
- **"You wondered about this once":** her old questions resurface on lazy evenings.

## 4. What this changes in onboarding (for Riya)
- **Spark choice:** her starter sparks should come from her top cells: *Why × mechanism*, *Why × story*, *Scale*, *Myth*. For example "Why do cats purr?", "Why are train coaches blue and red?", "How big is a blue whale's heart?", "Do we only use 10% of our brain?"
- **The guess-first hook (Direction 3) is perfect for her:** scale and myth questions *are* guess-first by nature.
- **With an import (Direction 1),** the Wrapped chapter can be her **question fingerprint**: "You're a *why* person: 64% of your questions start with why. You love hidden stories behind everyday things."
- **The first Exploration's lead card** is chosen from the intent × shape of her question. Her ending is rabbit holes + a share card (never a quiz first).
- **Tone:** playful, surprising, short sentences, one "no way" fact per card.
- **Don't** open with levels, streaks, a quiz, or "set your learning goal".
