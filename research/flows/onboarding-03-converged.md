## B. Jobs to be done, in detail

Format: **When** (situation), **I want to** (motivation), **so I can** (outcome). Then the functional, emotional and social layers, how they judge success, what makes them drop ChatGPT for us ("fire"), and what makes them drop us.

### Riya · The Wanderer

**Main job:** When something in the world makes me go "wait, why?", I want to understand it in a way that feels like a small adventure, so I can enjoy the "whoa" and share it.

**Related jobs**
1. When I'm scrolling or watching and a question pops up, I want to capture it in one tap, so I don't lose it.
2. When I've understood something, I want an obvious next thread to pull, so the curiosity keeps flowing.
3. When I learn something cool, I want something beautiful to send, so friends see what I found.
4. When I'm bored in the evening, I want something interesting already waiting, so I don't default to doomscrolling.
5. When I wondered about something weeks ago, I want to be reminded, so those questions don't die.

**Layers**
- **Emotional:** feel delighted, surprised, alive. Feel interesting.
- **Social:** be the friend who knows the cool fact.

**Success criteria (desired outcomes)**
- From question to "whoa" in under 30 seconds.
- Zero effort to go one level deeper.
- It looks good enough to screenshot.
- Never feels like homework.

**Fires ChatGPT because:** walls of text, no beauty, no "next", nothing to share.
**Fires us if:** it feels like school (levels, quizzes first, streak guilt), it's slow, or the visuals are generic AI art.

### Sam · The Builder

**Main job:** When I need to get up to speed on something for work, I want a clear mental model and the key facts I can trust, so I can hold my own and decide well tomorrow.

**Related jobs**
1. When I hit jargon in a meeting or doc, I want it explained in my context (my industry, my role), so it's immediately usable.
2. When a topic matters, I want to go deeper over a few days without losing the thread, so my understanding compounds.
3. When I'll be challenged on it, I want sources and the "why", so I can defend what I say.
4. When I've learned it, I want a crisp summary or framing I can reuse in a doc, slide or conversation.
5. When I'm busy, I want it fast and skimmable, so it fits into 5-minute gaps.

**Layers**
- **Emotional:** feel competent, prepared, ahead, never caught out.
- **Social:** look sharp in front of peers and leaders.

**Success criteria (desired outcomes)**
- A usable mental model in under 2 minutes.
- Visible sources and confidence.
- Examples from *his* world.
- Picks up exactly where he left off.

**Fires ChatGPT because:** no structure across days, hard to verify, forgets his context, nothing to revisit.
**Fires us if:** it's cute or fluffy, slow, unsourced, or has gamification that wastes his time.

### Meera · The Student

**Main job:** When a concept doesn't click from class or the textbook, I want it explained in a way I can see and play with, so it finally makes sense and I can prove it in the exam.

**Related jobs**
1. When I'm stuck, I want it explained differently from how my teacher did (picture, analogy, step by step), so it fits how I think.
2. When I think I get it, I want to test myself immediately, so I know I *really* understand.
3. When exams approach, I want to revise what I learned without starting over, so it sticks.
4. When something is just interesting (beyond the syllabus), I want to explore it without guilt, so studying feels less like a chore.
5. When I'm on a budget, I want real value before paying, so I know it's worth it.

**Layers**
- **Emotional:** stop feeling stupid; feel smart, confident, ready.
- **Social:** be the one who can explain it to classmates.

**Success criteria (desired outcomes)**
- "Now I get it" within one interaction.
- Gets the check question right.
- Can revise in 2 minutes.
- Exam-level depth when needed.

**Fires ChatGPT because:** she reads it and still doesn't get it, nothing interactive, no practice, no memory.
**Fires us if:** paywall before value, shallow content, no way to practise.

## C. Three onboarding directions

### Shared base (from Wondering, adapted)
Wondering's skeleton works:
1. A character greets.
2. At most 3 quick, acknowledged questions.
3. The first lesson is generated before signup.
4. A sign-in gate ("your first lesson is one step away").
5. Notifications only after value.
6. Land ask-first.

**Our changes to it:**
- The brain replaces the mascot.
- The first Exploration is *shown*, not only promised.
- Sign-in and paywall come at the rabbit holes (peak desire), together.
- The questions feel like a moment, not a form.

**All three directions share:**
- The brain as the live centrepiece (idle → listening → thinking → forms the topic).
- Persona detection.
- A tailored first Exploration (lead card, ending and tone vary by persona).
- The sign-in + paywall moment.
- The notification ask.
- Landing on Ask home.

**They differ in one thing:** *where the first curiosity comes from.* That changes the wow.

| | **1 · "Seen"** | **2 · "Ask"** | **3 · "Wonder"** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curiosity comes from | **Your past** (what you've already asked) | **Your head, right now** | **What Cortex shows you** (a guided spark and a taste test) |
| Wow | *"It knows me."* Being seen | *"It drew my question."* Instant magic | *"It explains the way my brain works."* Surprise + fit |
| Time to wow | ~90s | ~15s | ~40s |
| Friction | High (leave and import) | Lowest | Low |
| Closest reference | Quest | ChatGPT / Perplexity | Wondering + Brilliant |
| Risk | Drop-off at import; privacy worry | Generic first answer; weak "being seen" | Our question, not theirs, at first |

### Direction 1 · "Seen": your past curiosity, revealed

1. **Hello.** Dark studio. The brain forms from scattered dots. "Every question you've ever asked says something about you."
2. **Where do you ask?** ChatGPT · Claude · Gemini · Google · "I don't." (The last option drops them into Direction 3.)
3. **Bring your questions.** A founder-letter card ("what we read, what we never do") → copy the prompt → open ChatGPT → paste back. Or upload a screenshot of the chat list.
4. **The meter fills.** An *understanding* meter charges as the import parses. Questions fly in as dots and settle into the brain's regions in subject colours (J1, J5).
5. **Curiosity Wrapped.** 3 cinematic chapters on coloured aurora, swiped vertically:
   - "You asked 214 things this year. **38 were pure curiosity.**"
   - "You're a **rabbit-holer**." *(persona: Wanderer, Builder or Student, inferred)*
   - "The question you keep coming back to…"
6. **Confirm.** "Looks like you explore mostly for **work**. Right?" One tap to confirm or change (J1, J6).
7. **Before / after.** Their unfinished question. Their ChatGPT answer shrinks into a grey block; the brain forms the topic, and the Exploration opens (J2).
8. **First Exploration** (tailored): hook → aha (interactive) → 1–2 cards → persona ending (share card / sources / quick check) (J2, J4).
9. **Rabbit holes → sign in + paywall.** "Keep going", a 3-day trial, × to skip to free (J3, J6).
10. **Notifications.** "I'll tell you when a question you never finished has a next chapter." (J5)
11. **Ask home.** The brain lit in their colours; 3 sparks from their history.

---

### Direction 2 · "Ask": your curiosity right now

1. **Hello.** Light studio. The brain idles and responds to touch. "What are you curious about?" A composer (type or voice) plus 3 sparks tailored by locale and time of day: one fun, one work-ish, one study-ish (J1 by choice).
2. **Ask.** They type, speak or tap. The brain listens (voice waves), thinks (neurons fire), then forms the topic (~8s) (J2).
3. **Quick answer card.** The one-line answer plus the visual aha, in about 3 seconds. Then [Explore it] [Quiz me].
4. **First Exploration.** The tone adapts to the spark they chose (fun → Wanderer, work → Builder, study → Student) (J2, J4).
5. **Teach it about you** (inline, after the wow). "Want answers shaped to you? Two quick taps": why do you explore? · how do you like it explained? The next card re-renders to fit (J1).
6. **Optional upgrade.** "Bring your ChatGPT questions and I'll find the ones you never finished." The meter + mini-Wrapped, if they accept (J5).
7. **Rabbit holes → sign in + paywall** (J3, J6).
8. **Notifications → Ask home.**

---

### Direction 3 · "Wonder": a guided spark, then your way

1. **Hello.** Light studio, with curiosity spheres drifting in (each a question + illustration). The brain is in the centre. "Let's see how your brain likes to learn."
2. **Guess first.** One surprising question with a slider: "How much blood does your heart pump in a day?" They guess → reveal "7,500 litres, a swimming pool a week." The brain forms a heart (J2; the pretesting effect).
3. **Which explanation clicks?** The same idea shown three ways (story, diagram, analogy) as swipeable cards. Tap one. "Got it. **You think in pictures.**" (J1)
4. **Why do you explore?** Joy · Work · Study, with an acknowledgement in the brain's voice ("Work. I'll use examples from your world.") (J1)
5. **Now yours.** "What's something you've always wondered about?" Composer + 3 sparks shaped by the previous two answers. The brain thinks → forms (J2).
6. **First Exploration**, rendered in their chosen explanation style, with the persona ending (J2, J4).
7. **Rabbit holes → sign in + paywall** (J3, J6).
8. **Notifications → Ask home** with the brain and sparks. Import is offered later from Memory.

## How each direction covers the jobs
| Job | 1 · Seen | 2 · Ask | 3 · Wonder |
|---|---|---|---|
| J1 Get personal | ★★★ import + inferred persona | ★★ spark choice + 2 taps later | ★★★ guess + taste test + why |
| J2 Understand better | ★★★ before/after on *their* question | ★★★ instant, their question | ★★ our question first, then theirs |
| J3 Next thread | ★★ | ★★ | ★★ |
| J4 Make it mine | ★★ | ★★ | ★★★ style matched |
| J5 Don't lose it | ★★★ brain filled from history | ★★ optional import | ★ |
| J6 Feel safe | ★★ (import worry, mitigated by the letter) | ★★★ | ★★★ |

## What we learn by prototyping all three
- **Is being seen (1) worth its friction** versus instant magic (2)?
- **Does a guided taste test (3) personalise better** than inferring from history (1)?
- **Likely end state:** Direction 2's speed as the default, with Direction 1's reveal as the upgrade moment, and Direction 3's taste test folded into Direction 2's step 5.
